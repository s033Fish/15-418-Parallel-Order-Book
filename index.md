# Parallel Order Book Matching Engine
**Spencer Fisher, Kevin Tang**

## Motivation
An order book is a live list of open buy and sell orders for a specific financial asset. Matching engines use order books as a ledger to pair buyers and sellers whenever there is an overlap in prices. Exchanges, such as Nasdaq and NYSE are responsible for hosting and managing the electronic order books where the orders reside. High-Frequency trading (HFT) firms rely on microsecond level speed to execute various strategies. As such, an exchange is motivated to achieve maximal performance of their order book matching engines to attract more active HFT traders to maximize exchange revenue and market liquidity.
